# Paper 3 BCL11A Stricter Audit

**Date:** 2026-05-03  
**Status:** Internal audit note, not a results claim

## Conclusion

`BCL11A_erythroid` is still **not** a clean second positive regulatory locus.

## What the stricter audit shows

- The bottom-5% LSSIM cohort exists.
- The source audit retains only 3 rows for a primary pilot.
- The full 11-row bottom-5% cohort is dominated by `other` labels and coding/splice HGVS annotations.
- Position-matched controls are available and behave similarly under the current local interpretation.

## Why this matters

The locus is useful as a pilot, but not yet safe as a second positive claim.

The current evidence is compatible with:

- broad erythroid mixed annotations,
- a small promoter-proximal pilot,
- and strong position dependence.

It is **not** yet compatible with a clean regulatory-only positive locus.

## Decision

- Do **not** promote BCL11A as the second positive locus.
- Keep it only as a pilot / audit case.
- Move to a frozen import candidate instead of inflating the BCL11A claim.
