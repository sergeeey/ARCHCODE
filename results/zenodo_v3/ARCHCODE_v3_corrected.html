ARCHCODE: A Falsification-First Framework for Evaluating 3D Chromatin Structural Scores in Variant Interpretation — A β-Thalassemia Case Study

Sergey V. Boyko
Independent Researcher, Almaty, Kazakhstan
Correspondence: sergeikuch80@gmail.com
ORCID: [register before resubmission]

Abstract

Background: Sequence-based predictors capture coding and canonical splice effects, but are not designed to explicitly model 3D chromatin topology. ARCHCODE is a physics-informed loop-extrusion simulation framework intended as an orthogonal, hypothesis-generating layer for structural variant interpretation. A central methodological question is whether structural scores like LSSIM (Local Structural Similarity Index) carry independent information beyond consequence category and regulatory-distance baselines.

Methods: We analyzed ClinVar HBB variants (n = 1,103; 353 pathogenic, 750 benign) with ARCHCODE structural metrics under a strict falsification-first validation protocol. Claims were constrained by canonical governance artifacts and explicit evidence tiers (SUPPORTED, EXPLORATORY, UNVERIFIED). A systematic matched-control audit was applied across five experiments: (1) HBB matched-category test, (2) regulatory-confound models (category + CTCF-distance), (3) BCL11A as a second locus, (4) GATA1 matched-category test, and (5) HUDEP-2 real Hi-C retest (GSM4873116).

Results: The residual-signal hypothesis was falsified in all five tested configurations. The headline AUC 0.977 is a consequence-category distribution artifact: naive Cohen's d (−2.67) collapses to −0.34 after category stratification. The only non-circular matched test (within-category, where both pathogenic and benign variants exist) shows AUC ≈ 0.52 (intronic, n = 667, Mann–Whitney p = 0.80) — indistinguishable from chance. LSSIM is 91% explained by consequence category alone (R² = 0.907) and is substantially further explained by distance to CTCF sites (within-intronic Spearman ρ = 0.74). Adding LSSIM to category + position does not improve label prediction (CV AUC: category 0.980 = category + LSSIM 0.980). BCL11A failed as a second positive locus. GATA1 showed wrong-direction LSSIM in the missense category (AUC 0.46). HUDEP-2 real Hi-C (5kb resolution) placed all 1,103 HBB variants in a single bin (r = 0.16 between analytical and real contact maps, p = 0.30, not significant).

Conclusions: ARCHCODE does not currently demonstrate independent 3D chromatin pathogenicity signal in any tested configuration. The contribution is a systematic falsification framework: a method for auditing chromatin-based variant scores under category, positional, tissue-match, and resolution controls. The framework identifies exactly what is required for a fair test: ≤1 kb Hi-C from the correct erythroid tissue, regulatory non-coding variants, and pre-registered matched controls. The engine remains a valid hypothesis-generation tool for experimental prioritization under these constraints.

Limitations:

  • LSSIM is a deterministic function of consequence category (CATEGORICAL_EFFECTS lookup in source); the structural signal is not independent of categorical annotation
  • All tested atlases are built on analytical (mean-field) contact maps or K562 CTCF (wrong tissue for erythroid genes)
  • HUDEP-2 Hi-C extracted at 5 kb — too coarse for HBB intra-gene discrimination
  • Kramer kinetics parameters (α, γ) are manually calibrated, not fitted to experimental FRAP data
  • No wet-lab, RNA-seq, or CRISPR perturbation validation
  • Pearl variants are computational hypotheses only; no experimental confirmation
  • Hi-C validation: r = 0.16 (analytical vs real HUDEP-2, p = 0.30, not significant)

Keywords: β-thalassemia, HBB, chromatin loops, loop extrusion, cohesin, SSIM, falsification framework, matched controls, regulatory variants, variant interpretation, mean-field simulation, negative result
